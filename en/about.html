CIRTA is a nonprofit corporation dedicated to research and technological development.

Mission

"To create and apply scientific and technological knowledge to generate innovations with tangible and sustainable impact, promoting equitable and collaborative progress among companies, universities, and society."

Vision

"We aspire to a fairer, more resilient, and more connected society, where scientific and technological progress contributes to sustainable development, social inclusion, a competitive economy, and an ethical environment that promotes the common good."

What we do

We develop and implement solutions in robotics and applied artificial intelligence, with a focus on [e.g.: agriculture, education, and industrial automation]. Our programs include:

  • Development of technology platforms (simulation and applied robotics).
  • Execution of R&D projects with companies and universities.
  • Training and education in robotics and emerging technologies.
