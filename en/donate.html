CIRTA is a non-profit organization. Donations allow us to keep EduRobotics free, bring workshops to schools and communities, and move forward with research projects with social impact.

What does your donation fund?

  • Free education: maintaining and improving the EduRobotics simulator, which is and will remain free for students and educators.
  • Workshops: bringing hands-on robotics workshops to schools, universities and organizations.
  • Applied research: developing projects such as Zorzal and ROV maritime monitoring.

How to donate

For security reasons, we don't publish our bank details on the website. Write to us through the contact form, choosing the “Donations” reason, and we'll send them to you directly.
