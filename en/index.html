Our team's experience

Robotics projects that CIRTA members have taken part in at other institutions. They are not CIRTA projects: they show the experience we can bring to your company.

  • Sony AI

    ACE: table tennis robot

    Autonomous robot that combines event-based sensing and deep reinforcement learning to play against elite players. The research made the cover of Nature.

    Image: Sony AI

  • LEMUR Lab, Universidad de Chile

    Robot dog with a robotic arm

    Quadruped robot dog from the Mechatronics and Robotics Laboratory at Universidad de Chile, which is being fitted with a robotic arm.

    Image: LEMUR Lab

  • UChile Homebreakers

    Bender: service robot

    Humanoid service robot for human-robot interaction in homes: it recognizes faces and voice, handles objects and navigates around obstacles. It competes in RoboCup@Home.

    Image: UChile Homebreakers

  • UChile Homebreakers

    Maqui: Pepper robot

    Pepper humanoid robot programmed for human-robot interaction in homes. With it, the team won 1st place at RoboCup@Home SSPL in 2021.

    Image: UChile Homebreakers

  • Manipulation

    UR5 cobots with Robotiq grippers

    Object manipulation with UR5 collaborative arms and Robotiq grippers, the same type of robot we use in our EduRobotics simulator.

    Reference image: Universal Robots / Robotiq

  • Industrial robotics

    Industrial robots

    Programming and operating industrial robot arms for manipulation tasks.

    Reference image: KUKA

  • Bimanual manipulation

    Dual-arm robots

    Robots that coordinate two arms to handle objects, the way a person would with both hands.

    Reference image

  • Underwater robotics

    ROVs for monitoring

    Operating remotely operated underwater vehicles (ROVs) for underwater monitoring tasks.

    See maritime monitoring

    Reference image
