3. Who we share your data with

We do not sell or transfer your data. To run the site we use these providers, which process data on our behalf:

  • Google (Google Workspace, Apps Script, Sheets, Gmail and Google Fonts): receives and stores the forms, the newsletter and the assistant log, and serves the site's fonts.
  • Anthropic (Claude API): processes the messages you write to the assistant to generate replies.
  • Cloudflare (Turnstile): checks that whoever submits the forms is not a bot.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages): hosts the website.

If you choose to book a meeting from the assistant, you will be taken to Calendly, which handles your data under its own privacy policy.

6. Your rights

You can ask us at any time for:

  • Access: to know what data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Rectification: to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: to have us delete your data.
  • Objection: to have us stop using it for a specific purpose.
  • Portability: to receive your data in a format you can take elsewhere.
  • Blocking: to have us temporarily suspend the use of your data, for example while we review a request for rectification, erasure or objection.

You can also withdraw your consent whenever you want. Write to us at contacto@cirta.cl saying what you need; if it is about a conversation with the assistant, tell us the date, the approximate time and the page so we can find it. Exercising your rights is free.

We will reply within 30 calendar days, a period we may extend only once by another 30 calendar days if needed, and we will let you know. If we reject your request or do not reply in time, you can file a complaint with the Agencia de Protección de Datos Personales (Personal Data Protection Agency).

You also have the right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling. As explained in section 2, we do not make decisions of that kind.

8. Children and adolescents

The forms on this site are intended for adults. If you are under 14, ask your mother, father or guardian to write to us. If you take part in a workshop through your school or organization, the institution coordinates the necessary data with us.

9. Security

We limit access to your data to the team members who need it, and we use protected accounts and bot verification on the forms. If an incident affects your data, we will report it to the Agencia de Protección de Datos Personales (Personal Data Protection Agency) and, when appropriate, to you.
