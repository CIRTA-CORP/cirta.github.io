Challenge

Inspecting what happens underwater usually requires divers or expensive equipment, which limits how often and how widely coastal areas and marine structures can be monitored.

The pilot currently focuses on ship inspection: checking the condition of the hull below the waterline, a task that usually requires divers or dry-docking the vessel.

Solution

CIRTA works with a Chasing M2 S ROV, a remotely operated underwater vehicle that records video and images underwater without the need for divers.

With the ROV we carry out underwater visual inspections of ship hulls, recording video and images to assess their condition.

Training

Our ROV experience also reaches the community: in our workshops, participants learn how an ROV works and operate it in simulated exploration missions.
