Summary

AgroBot is an intelligent robotic sorting and packaging platform for export fruits and vegetables, equipped with computer vision trained and fine-tuned for the target harvest. It automates the identification of quality, size, and ripeness, enabling real-time sorting and uniform, efficient packaging of the products.

The platform incorporates an in-house robotic arm currently under development, designed specifically for the task of picking fruits and vegetables, optimized to reduce costs and speed up processes, allowing it to eventually scale to multiple units and improve efficiency for SMEs.
