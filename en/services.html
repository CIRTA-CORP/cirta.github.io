We work with companies, universities, public agencies and social organizations to design and implement technology solutions. As a nonprofit, we aim for every project to have a concrete and lasting impact.

What we do

Robotic prototypes

We design and build robotic platforms tailored to the problem, from idea to working prototype.

Example: Zorzal, with an in-house robotic arm under development.

Computer vision

We train models that recognize, classify and measure objects in images and video to automate inspections.

Example: sorting fruit by quality, size and ripeness in Zorzal.

Underwater inspection

Underwater monitoring and inspection with a remotely operated vehicle (ROV).

Example: maritime monitoring.

Applied R&D

Joint research and development projects with companies, universities and public organizations.

Training

Hands-on robotics workshops for schools, universities and organizations, using the EduRobotics simulator.

See workshops.

How we work

Each project moves through these stages according to its scope and needs.

  1. Initial conversationYou tell us about your challenge and we help you define it, at no cost.
  2. ProposalWe present an approach, stages, timelines and what we need from you.
  3. PrototypeWe build a first version to validate the solution.
  4. PilotWe test the prototype in real conditions and adjust based on your results.
  5. HandoverWe deliver the solution, documentation and training so your team can use it.
