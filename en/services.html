How we can work together

Consulting and integration

For companies that want to bring robotics, computer vision or artificial intelligence into their processes.

What you get

  • Technology assessment of your processes.
  • Integration of robotics, vision and AI solutions.
  • Training your team to operate them.

Professional service, quoted according to scope, with timelines agreed in the proposal. As a non-profit, we reinvest revenue in free robotics education.

Collaborative projects

For universities, public bodies and institutions that want to develop an R&D project together.

What you get

  • Co-design of the project and its goals.
  • A team experienced in applied robotics and AI.
  • Results with social, educational or productive impact.

What we expect from you

  • Joint fundraising (competitive grants, co-funding or partner contributions).

Challenges for the CIRTA community

For non-profits and early-stage startups.

What you get

  • A team of learners, guided by CIRTA professionals, that explores your challenge.
  • A proof of concept or prototype as the outcome.

What we expect from you

  • A contact person.
  • Information about the problem and regular feedback.
  • A contribution for basic materials and a donation to the organization.

It is built by volunteers in training, guided by professionals and backed by CIRTA's guarantee that the prototype will work as agreed. Since it is volunteer work, there is no set development timeline: if you need one, see Consulting. Places are limited.

How we work in consulting and collaboration

  1. Initial conversationYou tell us about your challenge and we help you define it, free of charge.
  2. ProposalWe present an approach, stages and timelines. For consulting, it includes the quote.
  3. PrototypeWe build a first version to validate the solution.
  4. PilotWe test the prototype in real conditions and adjust based on your results.
  5. Hand-offWe deliver the solution, documentation and training so your team can use it.

How challenges work

  1. SubmissionTell us about your organization and your challenge through the form.
  2. SelectionWe check whether it fits what our learners can learn from it and reply within 2 days with a time estimate. Since the work is done by volunteers, the development timeline is not fixed.
  3. Work with learnersA team of learners, guided by professionals, works on your challenge and shows you progress.
  4. Wrap-upWe deliver the proof of concept or prototype and what we learned. If both sides are interested, it can become a collaborative project.
